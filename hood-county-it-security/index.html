How we avoid the common MSP complaints

Reviews of managed providers keep returning to slow responses, surprise fees, and vague promises. Here is how we handle each.

  • Response: we state response targets in writing before you sign.
  • Billing: you get an itemized proposal that names what is included and what would cost extra.
  • Scope: we describe in plain terms what we cover, instead of promising unlimited everything.

Ask us for any of this in writing. You should ask every provider you are considering.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve businesses in Granbury and Hood County?

Yes. We are a Texas based remote managed provider serving Granbury, Hood County and DFW, plus clients elsewhere in the US. Nearly all support, security reviews and compliance work happen remotely, which keeps costs predictable. Call 469-248-5463 to talk through your setup.

Can a remote IT company really support a local business?

For most small business needs, yes. Help desk, Microsoft 365, backups, endpoint security, patching and compliance reviews can all be done remotely, often faster than scheduling a visit. For hardware that needs hands on work, we plan that with you or coordinate a local technician.

What should I ask an IT provider before I sign?

Ask for written response times, an itemized proposal showing what triggers extra charges, how they report on tickets, and what security tools are included. Ask for average response and resolution times from the past 6 to 12 months. A provider who does not track these is a warning sign.

Can you help with cyber insurance or an audit?

Yes. We run a Cyber Insurance Readiness Check before your application or renewal, and pre audit readiness checks before real auditors arrive or after SOC findings. We help you fix gaps and organize evidence. We cannot guarantee coverage, premiums or audit outcomes.

How much does IT security cost for a small business?

It depends on seats, tools and the level of support. Our fixed price Cyber Insurance Readiness Check starts at $995 for 5 to 15 seats. Readiness kits start at $49. Ongoing managed IT and security is quoted after a short review, with the price in writing up front.

Do you only work with very small companies?

No. We support businesses from small offices to enterprise teams, remotely. Smaller firms often start with a readiness check or kit, while larger ones may need compliance support or pre audit work. Call 469-248-5463 and describe your size and goals, and we will suggest a fit.
