FAQ

Questions People Usually Ask

Do I really need a CISO if I'm a small business?

Probably not a full-time one. But you do need someone accountable for security — someone who knows what to check and what to do when something goes wrong. That's exactly what a fractional CISO provides. Senior-level security leadership for a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.

My business is small. Are we really a target?

Yes — more so than you might think. The majority of ransomware attacks target businesses with fewer than 250 employees. Small businesses typically have weaker defenses. You don't have to be a household name to be worth attacking.

We already use a managed IT service (MSP). Do we still need you?

Quite possibly. Most MSPs are excellent at keeping systems running. But security compliance, risk assessments, HIPAA documentation, and strategic security planning are a different skillset — and most MSPs don't specialize there. I often work alongside existing MSPs and they welcome the support.

How does the HIPAA risk assessment work?

I conduct a structured review of how your practice creates, receives, stores, and transmits patient information — your technical controls, policies, staff training, physical safeguards, and vendor relationships. You receive a formal risk assessment document you can show an auditor. Typically delivered in 1-2 weeks.

What if we have a security incident or breach?

Call me. Retainer clients get priority response — I know your environment. For non-retainer clients I offer emergency incident response services. The first step is stopping the bleeding and preserving evidence — that's exactly where I start.

Do you work outside of Texas?

Most of my work is remote, so geography is rarely a barrier. I'm based in Granbury and most active in the DFW area for on-site work — but I work with clients across the country for remote assessments and retainer services.
